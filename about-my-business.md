# About my business

Interview answers, 30 September 2026.

## 1. Business and role
UshKa creates bold, avant-garde events. Megan Nakra is the Founder and CEO.

The events vary in style. They are for small and medium-sized companies that are advancing the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Every event is built around **impact, connection and memorable experiences**.

## 2. Who I make things for
UshKa is new, so for now the audience is everyone. In the next month the first readers are a group of **potential partners and clients** from:
- the private sector
- nonprofit organisations
- startups
- government organisations

The work supports first conversations and the building of warm relationships.

## 3. The work I hate making most
**The first draft of a deck.** I want to listen to clients and set the strategy and design direction out loud, without touching the keyboard until a first draft exists. I am the final reviewer and sign-off, with at least one or two rounds of review.

## 5. Words I never want to see
No consulting jargon, for example:
- "10,000 foot level"
- "boil the ocean"
- "synergize"
- "circle back"
- "AI-first"

Close relatives also stay out: "leverage", "move the needle", "low-hanging fruit".
